Used cooking oil, collected

Don't pour it down the drain. Hand it to someone who can use it.

Eco Oil Connect links hotels and cloud kitchens that have used cooking oil with collectors who pick it up and deliver it to biofuel companies. Register, raise a request, and follow the oil from your kitchen to the processor.

One request, four stops

Every request moves through the same stops, and its status shows which one it has reached.

  1. Restaurant registers

    A hotel or cloud kitchen enters its name, address, contact details and an estimate of its used oil.

  2. Collection request

    The restaurant submits a request with the quantity, container and a pin on the map.

  3. Collector handles the oil

    A collector reviews the request, accepts it and collects the oil.

  4. Biofuel company receives it

    The collected oil goes to a suitable biofuel company for processing.

What goes in the can

Collectors can only use clean used cooking oil. Mixed loads are often refused.

Accepted

  • Used frying oil from the kitchen or fryer
  • Oil that has been cooled and strained
  • Oil in a sealed, labelled container

Keep out

  • Engine oil, machine oil or any mineral oil
  • Water, soup, gravy or leftover sauce
  • Soap, detergent or cleaning chemicals
