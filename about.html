About us

The problem and our answer

The problem

Hotels and cloud kitchens fry in volume, so they end up with a lot of used cooking oil. There is often no easy, known way to hand it over. Oil poured into drains can block pipes and pollute water, and oil kept in storage takes up space.

Collectors and biofuel companies, meanwhile, struggle to find out where oil is waiting and how much of it there is.

Our solution

Eco Oil Connect puts both sides on one platform. A restaurant registers and raises a collection request. A collector sees the request on a Google map, accepts it and picks the oil up. The oil is then supplied to a biofuel company for processing.

Each request carries a status, so everyone can see whether it is waiting, accepted, picked up or delivered.

Who does what

Restaurant

Registers, lists the oil it has, and tracks each request.

Collector

Reviews requests, accepts them, collects the oil and delivers it.

Admin

Manages users and requests and reads messages from the Contact page.
